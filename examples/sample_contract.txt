MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. The Supplier will send an invoice at the end of every calendar quarter, and the Purchaser must settle each invoice in full within thirty (30) days of receiving it. Overdue sums carry interest at 1.5% per month.

2. Neither party will reveal the other party's trade secrets, pricing, customer lists or technical data to anyone outside its own staff who need to know it, and each party will protect that material using the same care it uses for its own sensitive information.

3. This Agreement is to be interpreted under, and governed by, the laws of Karnataka, India.

4. Any disagreement between the parties that cannot be settled by discussion within twenty-one days shall be referred to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat of arbitration in Bengaluru.

5. The Supplier's maximum aggregate liability for all claims under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid by the Purchaser in the six months before the event giving rise to the claim, and neither party is liable for lost profits or consequential loss.

6. The Supplier shall defend and hold the Purchaser harmless from all third-party claims, damages and legal costs arising from the Supplier's breach of this Agreement or its negligent acts.

7. The Purchaser may end this Agreement at any time by giving sixty days' written notice, and either party may end it straight away if the other commits a material breach that remains uncured fourteen days after notice.

8. Neither party will be in breach for any delay caused by fire, flood, pandemic, war, terrorism, strikes or other circumstances outside its reasonable control.

9. The Purchaser shall not transfer any of its rights under this Agreement to a third party unless the Supplier has first agreed in writing.

10. The Supplier warrants that all services will be carried out with reasonable skill and care by suitably qualified personnel.
